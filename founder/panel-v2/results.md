## What would flip a no

- If the free summary actually got a doctor to take me seriously and order the PCOS workup, I might pay the $7.99 once for my follow-up appointment.
- If the free PDF turned out to be too thin to be useful at my appointment and the $7.99 one-off was clearly better, I might pay once. A subscription, never.
- If the free summary actually got my doctor to change something or answer a question she normally brushes off, I'd pay the $7.99 before my next appointment.
- If my doctor actually used the free summary at my next appointment and it saved me from explaining my whole history again, I'd pay the $7.99 for the one after that.
- If the free summary actually showed my doctor whether metformin or inositol is doing anything, I'd pay the $7.99 before my next appointment.
- If I'm still logging after a month and the free summary actually changes how my appointment goes, like my doctor finally takes the irregular cycles seriously or orders the right tests, I'd pay the $7.99 for my next appointment.
- If my doctor actually changed something because of the free summary, I might pay the $7.99 one-time for the next appointment. I'd never do a subscription.
- An independent privacy audit or open-source proof that nothing leaves the phone, and a free summary that actually helps my doctor change something about my metformin or inositol plan.
- Getting a real PCOS appointment on the calendar. Then the $7.99 one-off with no subscription is something I'd actually pay, as long as the privacy policy holds up when I read it.
- If the free summary actually made my appointment go differently, like getting a test ordered or a med change taken seriously, I'd pay the $7.99 before the next one.
- If the free summary actually got me taken seriously, like a doctor ordering the bloodwork or ultrasound instead of brushing me off, I'd pay the $7.99 for my next appointment.
- If the free summary actually made my doctor change something, like adjusting the metformin or ordering labs, I'd pay the $7.99 before the next appointment.
