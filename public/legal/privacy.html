1. Who we are and what this covers

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("Solia Health", "we", "us") makes the Solia Health apps and website (the "Service"). This policy explains what information the Service handles, where it goes, and your choices. It is part of our Terms of Use. If you live in Washington, Nevada or Connecticut, our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy also applies to you.

Solia Health is a consumer wellness app. We are not a hospital, clinic, doctor or health plan, and we do not act for one, so the federal health privacy law HIPAA does not apply to the Service. State laws do, and we follow them, including California's Confidentiality of Medical Information Act as it applies to reproductive and sexual health apps, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Washington's My Health My Data Act.

2. What stays on your device

The Service is built "local-first". The following is stored only on your phone or in your browser's storage and is never sent to us:

  • Your daily check-ins, symptoms, moods, energy, sleep, pain and other scores, and day notes
  • Your cycle and period log, fertility signs, pregnancy details and kick counts, and life stage
  • The intimacy and relationship log, journal entries, appointments, and medicines, supplements and other things you are trying
  • The optional add-on logs (for example POTS heart rate, blood pressure, episodes, drinks and salt, and exercise sessions)
  • Patterns, predictions, milestones and reports the app works out from your log
  • Your optional name, email, age band and ZIP code, if you enter them on the device
  • Data read from Apple Health or Android Health Connect, if you allow it
  • Your settings, theme and app lock PIN

Because it stays on your device, we cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if your device is lost or reset. Files you export yourself (such as a PDF summary, a spreadsheet or an encrypted backup file) go only where you send them, and the apps you send them to may keep copies under their own terms.

4. How we use information

We use the limited information that reaches us only to: provide the features you turn on; keep your purchases working; keep the Service secure and stop fraud and abuse; answer you; comply with law; and enforce our Terms. We use health information only to provide the feature you asked for. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any purpose you have not consented to.

5. Who receives information

  • Service providers that process information for us under contract: Render (hosting), Anthropic (AI Features you turn on), RevenueCat (subscription status), and Terra and Oura (connections you choose). They may use it only to provide their service to us or to you.
  • Apple and Google, when you buy through their stores or sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal and safety: only as described in Section 7.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information we hold may be transferred, but it remains subject to this policy, and health information will not be transferred without the consent the law requires.
  • With your direction: when you choose to share something, such as sending a summary to your doctor.

6. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal information or health information, and we never "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as the CCPA defines those terms.
  • We never use your health information for advertising or give it to advertisers, data brokers or insurers.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and fonts are built in, so no third party is contacted when you simply use the app.
  • We never use your information to train AI models, and we do not allow our AI provider to do so under our agreement [confirm in the Anthropic agreement].
  • We never set up geofences around health care facilities.

7. Legal requests and reproductive health

Because your log stays on your device, we hold very little that could be demanded from us. We will disclose information we hold only when we believe in good faith that the law requires it. We will:

  • review every request and push back on requests that are overbroad, invalid or seek information about lawful reproductive or sexual health care;
  • not provide information in response to a request from another state relating to reproductive health care that is lawful in California, to the extent California law forbids it;
  • tell you about a request before disclosing, unless the law or a court order forbids it; and
  • disclose only the minimum required.

We may also disclose information we hold if needed to protect someone from imminent serious harm, to protect the security of the Service, or to enforce our Terms.

8. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above: as listed in Section 3, or longer only where the law requires it. Information on your device stays until you delete it, using Delete everything in the app or by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data.

9. Security

Connections to our server use TLS encryption. Backups are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from your passphrase using PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 rounds); we never receive the passphrase. You can add an app lock PIN. On the web, the app lock keeps the app closed but does not encrypt the browser's storage, so protect your computer's account and browser. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information on your device, in files you export, or in transit over networks we do not control.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Turn features off at any time in Settings. Turning off reminders deletes the reminder record on our server.
  • Delete everything in Settings erases the record on your device and ends server-side reminders and wearable connections. AI questions already sent and counts already added cannot be deleted for one person, as the app explains.
  • Delete your account and cloud backup in the app at any time; both are removed at once.
  • Health permissions for Apple Health and Health Connect can be changed in your phone's settings.
  • Privacy requests. You can ask to access, delete or correct personal information we hold, or appeal a decision, by emailing [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days (we may extend once by 45 days and will tell you why). We verify requests using information you give us; because we hold so little, we may not be able to link a request to any information. You can use an authorized agent with your signed permission. We will not discriminate against you for using your rights.
  • Global Privacy Control. We treat a GPC signal as a request to opt out of sale and sharing. We already do neither.
