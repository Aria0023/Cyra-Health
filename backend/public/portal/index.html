Solia Health Partner portal

Business information only: brands, products, the people who edit them, anonymous daily totals of Shop taps per product, and for Solia Health's owners, counts of accounts and sign-ins, affiliate network totals, a weekly report of those totals, which settings are in place on the server, an estimate of what the AI costs from daily token totals, subscription numbers, promotions, free Pro grants by account email, partner codes, and the feedback people send from the app. Nobody's health log is ever shown or stored here. Feedback is free text: people are asked not to include health details, but a message may hold what its writer chose to type, so treat it as private.
