If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. If you are thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988.

Solia Health is not a medical device

Solia Health shows patterns from what you log and plain-language information. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition and does not replace your clinician. It has not been reviewed or cleared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

Not for birth control

Period, ovulation and fertile-window predictions are estimates and can be wrong. Do not use Solia Health to prevent pregnancy, or rely on it alone when trying to get pregnant.

Pregnancy

Pregnancy features do not monitor you or your baby. Contact your clinician or labor and delivery unit right away about fewer movements, bleeding, pain, leaking fluid, severe headache, vision changes, swelling or anything that worries you.

Medicines, supplements and "what I'm trying"

Tracking how you feel on a medicine, supplement or change does not show that it caused any improvement or harm. Patterns can be coincidence. Never start, stop or change a medicine or treatment without talking to your clinician or pharmacist.

Information and guidance

Articles, practices and guidance are general education drawn from sources we believe are reputable. Medicine changes, and information may be incomplete, out of date or not right for you. It is not personal medical advice.

AI features

Optional AI answers and write-ups are generated by a computer model. They can be wrong, incomplete or made up while sounding confident, and no clinician reviews them. Use them only as a starting point for a conversation with your clinician, never as a diagnosis or a reason to delay care.

Wearables and connected data

Readings from wearables and phone health apps can be inaccurate or missing. Solia Health does not check them and does not alert anyone if they look abnormal.

Care tab and outside services

Products, telehealth services and clinicians shown in the Care tab are run by others. Listing one is not a recommendation or a medical referral, and any care you get is between you and that provider.
