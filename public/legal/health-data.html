Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE] · For residents of Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, and anyone whose consumer health data is collected in Washington

This policy is required by Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373), Nevada's consumer health data law (SB 370) and Connecticut's consumer health data provisions. It supplements our Privacy Policy. "Consumer health data" means personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status, including reproductive and sexual health information. Even the fact that you use a period, fertility, pregnancy or menopause app can be consumer health data, so this policy covers it too.

1. Consumer health data we collect, and why

Most of your health information never reaches us: it stays on your device (see Section 2 of the Privacy Policy). We collect consumer health data only when you turn on a feature that sends it, and only for that feature:

CategoryPurpose and use
Questions you type to the AI, or a summary of your log you choose to send (may describe symptoms, cycles, pregnancy, medicines or sexual health)To answer your question or write the summary you asked for.
Life-stage group and yes/no symptom flags (anonymous weekly counts)To show everyone anonymous weekly totals. A total is shown only once it reaches 50 contributions.
Wearable readings (such as heart rate, sleep and temperature) from Oura or Terra-supported devicesTo deliver your readings from that service to your device.
The fact that you have an account, reminders or a purchase with a reproductive and women's health app, linked to an email hash, push address or anonymous purchase idTo provide accounts, encrypted backup, reminders and Pro.
Your encrypted backupTo store it for you. It is encrypted on your device and we cannot read it.

We use consumer health data only to provide the feature you asked for, and to keep the Service secure. We do not use it for advertising, and we never collect precise location or set geofences around health care facilities.

2. Where it comes from

From you (what you type or log and choose to send), from your device, and from the wearable and sign-in services you choose to connect (Oura, Terra, Apple, Google).

3. Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We share consumer health data only with processors who handle it for us under contracts that limit them to providing their service, and only for the features you turn on:

  • Anthropic, PBC: AI questions and summaries you choose to send.
  • Render Services, Inc.: hosting of our server, which carries the data listed above.
  • Terra and Oura: wearable connections you choose (they also act under their own policies with you).
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: subscription status (which can reveal that you use Solia Health), with no health details.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. [Update if that changes.] We do not sell consumer health data, and would never do so without your signed authorization as the law requires.

4. Consent

We collect and share consumer health data only with your consent, which you give separately for each feature when you turn it on, after the app tells you what it sends and to whom. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature off in Settings; collection then stops. 5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and access it, including a list of all third parties and affiliates we have shared it with and their contact details;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • have your consumer health data deleted, including by our processors; and
  • appeal our decision on your request.

How to exercise them: use the in-app controls (turn a feature off, Delete everything, delete your account) or email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We will respond within 45 days (extendable once by 45 days when reasonably necessary, with notice), free of charge up to twice a year. We will verify your request with commercially reasonable methods; because we keep so little, we may be unable to link some data to you. To appeal a decision, email [PRIVACY EMAIL] with "Appeal" in the subject within a reasonable time; we will answer within 45 days. If we deny your appeal, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov), the Nevada Attorney General (ag.nv.gov) or the Connecticut Attorney General (portal.ct.gov/ag).

Deleting data on your device, using Delete everything, deletes it immediately. We cannot delete AI questions already processed by Anthropic beyond its own retention period, or contributions already added to anonymous weekly totals, because they cannot be linked back to you.

6. Security and access

Only people who need consumer health data to provide or secure the Service may access it. Security measures are described in Section 9 of the Privacy Policy.
